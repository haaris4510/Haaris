About

the director's note

I'm Haaris, a first-year B.Tech student in Information Technology in Tamil Nadu. I came into college knowing that I liked computers, and not much else. Every week I find out a little more about what that actually means.

What I enjoy most is the moment something I wrote finally works after an hour of not working. I'm still early, so most of what I make is small. I'd rather show you small things that are honestly mine than big things I only half understand.

This site is one of them. I picked the look of a film title sequence because I like things that begin with a bit of ceremony and then get out of your way.
